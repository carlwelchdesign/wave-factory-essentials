# Product Backlog

## Existing plugins

### Threefold Palm

- Phase 1: gain-reduction meters, Auto Match, factory Forms
- Later: crossover controls, oversampling, expanded factory Forms, broader listening and CPU evaluation

### Valley Spirit

- Phase 1: tempo sync, Freeze, Reflection/Spiral/Cloud feedback Paths
- Later: pitch sequencing, key/scale constraints, envelope-driven movement, controlled randomness

## Future plugin concepts

### Reference Coach

A learning-focused tonal and dynamics comparison tool that helps beginners compare their vocal or mastering balance with genre references. It should teach listening rather than promise automatic imitation. AI/reference-licensing implications require a separate product and business decision.

### Topline Studio

A vocal-generation concept that places generated vocals over an imported beat. This explicitly crosses the non-AI boundary and therefore requires separate pricing, consent, rights, privacy, and infrastructure planning.

### Transforming reverb

A magical reverb whose tail can shift pitch without automation. This may become a Valley Spirit sibling after the delay architecture and feedback paths are mature.

## Selection rule

Finish and validate the current expansion before starting another plugin. A new concept should have a specific user problem, a clear non-goal, a testable DSP hypothesis, and a distinct place in the product mythology.
