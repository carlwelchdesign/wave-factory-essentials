# Product brief

## Promise

Wave Factory Essentials gives producers focused tools that solve concrete workflow problems without subscriptions, accounts, AI inference, or hidden audio uploads.

## Goodband

Goodband answers Presstige's request for a multiband “sound-good” processor that is not Maximus. Its differentiation is speed plus transparency: a producer should be able to improve energy and density quickly, level-match the result, and understand which bands are being changed.

Initial controls:

- Amount
- Character: Clean, Warm, Punch, Wide
- Mix
- Output trim

The current alpha implements a complementary three-band split, linked-stereo envelope control, character-dependent soft saturation, and output trim. Metering, oversampling, crossover controls, and a true loudness-matched audition mode remain future work.

## Pitch Trails

Pitch Trails answers Presstige's request for pitch-shifting reverb and delay that does not require drawn automation. Its differentiation is an internal musical-motion workflow rather than pitch shifting by itself.

Initial controls:

- Delay time
- Pitch in semitones
- Feedback
- Diffusion
- Mix

The current alpha implements dual crossfaded pitch-delay heads, bounded feedback, and a stereo diffusion tail. Tempo sync, step sequencing, scale constraints, modulation modes, freeze, and selectable feedback-path placement remain future work.

## Free-offer boundary

Both plugins are deterministic local DSP and qualify for the free non-AI member offer. They still carry ordinary distribution costs: development, testing, signing, notarization, packaging, maintenance, and support.

## Non-goals for the foundation

- generative audio or AI assistance
- cloud processing or user accounts
- emulating Maximus or another named plugin
- production-ready mastering claims
- bundled copyrighted audio or presets derived from unlicensed material
- AAX distribution before the required SDK and signing path are established
